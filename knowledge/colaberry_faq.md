# Colaberry Admissions & Program FAQ

_Last updated: 2025-01-01_  
_This FAQ is meant to help prospective students understand Colaberry’s data programs, admissions process, time commitment, and support._

---

## 1. About Colaberry

### What is Colaberry?

Colaberry is a training and career-transition program focused on **data analytics** and **data science**.  
We help motivated adults, often from non-technical backgrounds, gain practical, job-ready skills and transition into data roles such as:

- Data Analyst  
- Business Intelligence (BI) Developer  
- Data Engineer (entry-level)  
- Junior Data Scientist  

Our programs combine:

- Live, instructor-led sessions  
- Self-paced online content  
- Real-world portfolio projects  
- Career coaching and interview preparation  

---

### Who are Colaberry programs designed for?

Colaberry programs are designed primarily for:

- Career changers who want to move into **data analytics / BI / data science**  
- Working professionals who need a structured path to upskill in data  
- People with **little or no prior coding experience**, but strong motivation to learn  

Many students come from backgrounds like:

- Finance, accounting, operations, healthcare, customer service  
- Teaching, retail, hospitality, or other non-technical fields  

You do **not** need a computer science degree to succeed.  
What you do need is:

- Basic computer literacy  
- Comfort with learning new tools  
- Willingness to practice consistently each week  

---

### What makes Colaberry different from other bootcamps?

Key differences:

- **Industry-focused curriculum** — we teach the tools and workflows used every day in data jobs: SQL, Power BI, Python, data visualization, and more.  
- **Project-based learning** — you build real, end-to-end data projects you can showcase in your portfolio.  
- **Ongoing support** — even after you complete the program, you can access resources, workshops, and community support.  
- **Flexible schedule** — many learners study part-time while working full-time.

## 2. Programs & Courses

### What programs does Colaberry offer?

Program offerings may evolve, but most students join one of these tracks:

1. **Data Analytics Program**
   - Foundations of data analysis
   - SQL for querying databases
   - Power BI for dashboards and reporting
   - Excel for quick analysis and business scenarios

2. **Advanced Analytics / Data Science Program**
   - Python for data analysis and automation  
   - Statistics and probability for data work  
   - Machine learning basics (regression, classification, model evaluation)  
   - Working with real-world datasets end-to-end  

3. **Visualization & BI Focus (Power BI/Visualization)**
   - Designing compelling dashboards  
   - DAX fundamentals  
   - Data modeling for reporting  
   - Best practices for communicating insights to business stakeholders  

> Note: Program names and details can change. An admissions advisor can walk you through the current catalog and recommend the track that fits your goals.

---

### What skills will I learn in the data-focused programs?

In the core data programs, you can expect to learn:

- **SQL**
  - Writing SELECT queries and joins  
  - Filtering, grouping, and aggregations  
  - Working with real relational databases  

- **Power BI / Data Visualization**
  - Connecting to data sources  
  - Building dashboards and interactive visual reports  
  - Basics of DAX for calculations and measures  

- **Python (in advanced tracks)**
  - Data cleaning and transformation with libraries like pandas  
  - Exploratory data analysis (EDA)  
  - Intro-level machine learning workflows  

- **Statistics & Analytics Thinking**
  - Descriptive statistics (mean, median, variance, etc.)  
  - Working with distributions and sampling  
  - Turning business questions into analytical questions  

- **Real-World Projects**
  - End-to-end projects using real datasets  
  - Building a portfolio you can show to employers  

---

### Do you teach Power BI?

Yes. Power BI is a core tool in Colaberry’s **data analytics** and **BI-focused** programs.  
You’ll learn how to:

- Import and clean data in Power BI  
- Build dashboards and reports  
- Use basic DAX for measures and calculated columns  
- Publish and share dashboards with stakeholders  

---

### Do you teach data science or just analytics?

Colaberry’s main focus is **data analytics and BI**, but some tracks also cover **introductory data science concepts** such as:

- Python for data analysis  
- Building and evaluating basic machine learning models  
- Working with real datasets end-to-end  

If you’re specifically targeting **data scientist** roles, talk to an advisor about the most current advanced offerings and recommended path.

## 3. Time Commitment & Schedule

### How many hours per week do I need to commit?

Most Colaberry learners study **part-time while working**. A typical weekly commitment:

- **Live sessions:** 2–3 sessions per week (1–2 hours each)  
- **Self-paced work & practice:** 5–10 hours per week  

Overall, many students invest **8–12 hours per week**, depending on pace and goals.

---

### Can I do the program while working full-time?

Yes. The program is specifically designed so **working professionals** can participate:

- Live sessions usually happen in the **evenings or weekends** (depending on cohort and region)  
- A significant portion of the work is self-paced, so you can schedule it around your job  

An advisor can help you estimate whether the timeline and schedule will fit your life.

---

### How long does the program take?

Exact timelines vary by track, but as a rough guide:

- **Data Analytics Program:** ~3–6 months part-time  
- **Advanced / Data Science components:** may add another 2–4 months  

Some students take a bit longer if they need extra time for practice, projects, and job search preparation.

---

### When does the next cohort start?

Cohort start dates are updated regularly.  
A typical pattern is:

- New **data analytics** cohorts starting every few weeks  
- Advanced or specialized cohorts starting on a rotating schedule  

To get the next available start date for your program, you can:

- Fill out the online application form  
- Schedule a call with an admissions advisor

## 4. Admissions & Registration

### How do I get registered for a Colaberry program?

The typical registration process:

1. **Submit an online application form**  
   - Basic contact information  
   - Your background and goals  
   - Which program(s) you’re interested in  

2. **Talk with an admissions advisor**  
   - Review your career goals and experience  
   - Discuss which track fits you best  
   - Go over schedule, tuition, and expectations  

3. **Confirm your enrollment**
   - Choose start date and program  
   - Complete enrollment documents  
   - Set up your payment plan or tuition arrangements  

4. **Get onboarding details**
   - Access to learning platforms  
   - Orientation schedule  
   - Pre-course preparation steps  

---

### Is there an application deadline?

Yes, each cohort has an enrollment cutoff. For example:

- Many cohorts close **about one week before** the start date  
- Some seats may fill earlier if the cohort reaches capacity  

It’s best to **apply early**, especially if you need time to organize financing or adjust your schedule.

---

### Do I need prior coding or math background?

You do **not** need to be a programmer or mathematician to start. However, it helps if you:

- Are comfortable with basic computer use  
- Can work with spreadsheets (e.g., Excel or Google Sheets)  
- Are willing to practice consistently  

For data science–oriented tracks, some comfort with **algebra and statistics** is helpful. An advisor can recommend prep resources if you’re feeling rusty.

## 5. Tuition, Cost & Financial Support

### How much does it cost to register?

Tuition for Colaberry programs **varies by track and duration**. Exact pricing can change over time. In general:

- Data analytics / BI programs are **multi-thousand-dollar** intensive trainings  
- Advanced data science components may have additional tuition  

Instead of a one-size-fits-all answer, admissions will:

- Review your goals  
- Confirm the specific program package and schedule  
- Provide a **clear tuition breakdown** and payment options  

---

### What payment options are available?

Common options include:

- **Paying upfront** (often with the lowest total cost)  
- **Monthly installment plans** over the duration of the program  
- In some regions or situations, there may be **employer sponsorship** or other funding options  

Your admissions advisor can review **which options are currently available** and what the payment schedule would look like for you.

---

### Are scholarships available?

Scholarship availability can vary. In many cases:

- There may be **limited scholarships or discounts** for:
  - First-generation students  
  - Individuals from underrepresented backgrounds in tech  
  - People experiencing financial hardship  

These opportunities are typically competitive and may require:

- A short written statement  
- Basic financial information  
- Early application  

Ask your advisor about **current scholarship opportunities** when you apply.

## 6. Support, Mentoring & Career Services

### What kind of support will I get during the program?

Support includes:

- Access to **instructors and teaching assistants** during live sessions  
- Discussion forums or communication channels for questions between sessions  
- Project support and feedback on your work  
- Technical guidance on tools like SQL, Power BI, and Python  

---

### Do you help with the job search?

Yes. Career support typically includes:

- **Resume and LinkedIn review** tailored to data roles  
- Guidance on **portfolio projects** and how to present them  
- Practice **behavioral and technical interviews**  
- Tips for networking and applying for jobs  

While no program can guarantee a job, the goal is to prepare you to compete effectively for **entry-level data roles**.

## 7. Program Policies & Logistics

### How is the content delivered?

Most Colaberry programs are **fully online**, combining:

- Live virtual classes (via video conferencing)  
- Recorded sessions for review  
- Self-paced modules and exercises  
- Real-world projects and case studies  

---

### What happens if I miss a live class?

Life happens. If you miss a live session, you can typically:

- Watch the **recording**  
- Review slides and materials  
- Follow up with instructors or TAs for key questions  

Repeated absences can affect your progress, so it’s best to stay as engaged as possible.

---

### Is there an orientation?

Yes. Orientation is typically held **shortly before your program begins**. It usually covers:

- Platform walkthroughs (learning portal, communication tools)  
- Expectations and best practices  
- How to get help if you get stuck  
- Tips for balancing study with work and life  

---

### What if I need to pause or withdraw?

Policies can vary by program and region, but in general:

- If you need to **pause** for personal reasons, you may be able to defer to a later cohort, depending on timing and seat availability.  
- **Refund or cancellation policies** are usually outlined in your enrollment agreement.  

If you anticipate a schedule issue, talk to student support as early as possible.

1. **Submit a short application** online

   - Your background and goals  
   - Target job roles and timeline  
   - Tuition and payment options  
   - Weekly time commitment  

During that conversation, you’ll get a clearer sense of:

- Whether Colaberry is a good fit  
- Which program track matches your goals  
- What your path to a data career could look like
